About Endesk

One partner. Multiple business solutions.

ENDESK Conglomerate is a South African multipurpose service provider established in 2006 and registered in 2014.

Owned by Mr Anthony Osawaru Okundaye and Mrs Lungile Promise Okundaye, the company offers practical solutions across automotive, procurement, travel and visa assistance and related business support.

Our approach centres on understanding each client's requirements, connecting them with appropriate solutions and maintaining dependable working relationships.
